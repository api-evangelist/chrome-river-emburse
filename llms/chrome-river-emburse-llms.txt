# Chrome River (Emburse)

> Chrome River is an enterprise travel and expense (T&E) management platform, now part of Emburse. It provides expense reporting, invoice management, corporate card reconciliation, and travel spend controls for large and global organizations. The chromeriver.com brand redirects to Emburse Enterprise. Emburse exposes data through a gated API host (api.emburse.com) and the app.chromeriver.com application; no open developer portal or OpenAPI is published publicly. Backed by Bain Capital Ventures.
